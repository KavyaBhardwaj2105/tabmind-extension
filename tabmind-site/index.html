FEATURES

A gentle nudge, not a blocker.

TabMind doesn't lock you out of anything. It just makes your intentions hard to forget.

📝

Purpose prompt

Open a new tab, and TabMind asks what you're there for — "fix auth bug," "research Ladakh trip," anything short.

💭

Floating reason pill

Your answer stays visible as a small, minimal pill on the page — always in sight, never in the way.

✏️

Inline editing

Changed your mind mid-task? Click the pill, retype, hit Enter. Esc cancels. No menus, no friction.

⏱️

Focus timer

A quiet timer tracks how long you've lingered — useful for noticing drift before it becomes an hour.

🎚️

Full customization

Toggle the timer, toggle the pill, adjust opacity — changes apply instantly from the popup, no refresh.

🌙

Built to disappear

Dark-mode friendly, subtle gradients, translucent by design — it blends into any site instead of shouting.

HOW IT WORKS

Three small habits, one focused browser.

01

State your intention

The moment you open a tab, TabMind asks why — a five-second habit that pays for itself within the hour.

02

Keep it in view

Your reason floats as a small pill on the page itself, so you never have to hold it in your head alone.

03

Notice the drift

The focus timer quietly counts up. If it's climbing and the pill no longer matches what's on screen, that's your cue.

Your thoughts stay with you.

TabMind runs entirely inside your browser. There's no server to send your tab reasons to, because there's no server at all.

  • ✓ All data stored locally via Chrome Storage API
  • ✓ No tracking, no analytics
  • ✓ No ads, ever
  • ✓ No frameworks — vanilla JS, lightweight and fast
